# Olist — Shipping Partner Evaluation  

This repository presents the evaluation of the current shipping company used
by Olist, with the purpose of determining whether to continue the partnership
based on delivery performance, estimation accuracy, and cost efficiency.

---

## Objective
Assess the reliability and financial competitiveness of the current logistics
provider and decide whether the partnership should continue.

---

## Evaluation Pillars
The decision was based on four business dimensions:

1) **Status** : Early / On-time / Late delivery performance  
2) **Estimation** : Accuracy of estimated vs. actual delivery days  
3) **Distance** : Whether geography drives delays  
4) **Price** : Market cost comparison against other shipping companies  

---

## Executive Summary — Key Findings

- **91% of deliveries arrive early** → strong operational reliability  
- **Late deliveries are rare** and mainly caused by estimation and dispatch delays  
- **Very long-distance routes are underestimated**  
- **Current provider offers the lowest cost** in the market (R$ 12–31 vs. 30–150+)  

---

## Recommendation

- **Continue partnering** with the current shipping company  
- **Adjust estimated delivery days** for very long-distance routes  
- **Monitor long-distance lanes** to track improvements over time  

The partner is cost-efficient and performs reliably; delays stem from
expectation-setting (estimation), not from logistics performance itself.
